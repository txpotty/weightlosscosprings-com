    How Sildenafil Works in Colorado Springs

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. That enzyme normally breaks down cGMP, the molecule that relaxes smooth muscle in the blood vessels of the penis, so blocking it lets blood flow in more easily during arousal. Arousal is still required. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. It makes it easier to get an erection and keep it firm enough for sex.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. It works whether the cause is blood flow, nerves, stress or some mix. Men with diabetes or high blood pressure often respond well, though those conditions need their own care too. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. That is usually men who want a longer window.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Colorado Springs

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Tablets come in 25, 50 and 100 mg, with 50 mg as the usual starting point. One dose per 24 hours, maximum. A greasy dinner can push the onset back by an hour, so a lighter meal beforehand helps. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need several attempts before the timing clicks. Providers generally suggest trying a dose on four to eight separate occasions before calling it a failure, since nerves and alcohol can throw off the first attempt or two. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Jot down the dose, how long before sex you took it and how it went. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Colorado Springs

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. Some men notice a blue tint to their vision or extra light sensitivity for a few hours. Alcohol and blood pressure pills both make dizziness more likely, especially at 100 mg and especially when standing up fast. Sudden vision loss, sudden hearing loss or an erection lasting more than four hours needs emergency care. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. Nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers are all nitrates, and pairing any of them with sildenafil can crash blood pressure. Riociguat is also off the table. Questions cover recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain that comes on at rest and serious liver or kidney problems. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Colorado Springs

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, or can be taken every day at a low dose. Chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets are compounded formats, and compounded products are not FDA-approved. Because the generic is FDA-approved and identical in active ingredient to Viagra, providers often start here when a man wants a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
